## Video carousel ordering

When adding a video to Individual Performances or Full Recitals, insert it before the first existing entry of that category in `videos.json`. The new video becomes number 1; all existing videos shift one position, retaining their relative order. Do not append new videos at the end. Explicit user ordering requests take precedence.

The current Individual Performances order starts with Schumann: Fast zu ernst and ends with Adon Olam.
